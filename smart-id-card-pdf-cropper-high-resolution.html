How selection works
  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Click Pin 4 corners.
  3. Click the four corners in this order:
    • Top-left
    • Top-right
    • Bottom-right
    • Bottom-left
  4. The tool connects the four points with a green border.
  5. Assign the selection as Front or Back.
  6. Repeat for the other side and download your PDF.

You can use Reset corners to start a selection again.

Print settings

Standard ID-1 card size is 85.60 × 53.98 mm. Other ID documents may use different dimensions. Choose the correct size for your document.

Print layout preview

Select both Front and Back to preview your print layout.
When printing, select “Actual size” or “100%” and disable “Fit to page”. Verify the dimensions with a test print.
